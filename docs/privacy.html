Three Little Circles

Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026

Three Little Circles is a field guide for spotting hidden details in theme parks. It works without an account, and almost everything it knows about you never leaves your phone.

This is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any theme park company.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, and no email address are required to use the app.
  • Your progress, badges, and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Your location is used on your phone to place you on the map. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to submit a sighting or report whether a find is still there.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following in its own private storage, which is removed when you delete the app:

  • Which finds you have marked as found, and when.
  • Which badges you have earned.
  • Your appearance and map preferences.
  • Which entries you have reported on.
  • A random identifier generated on first launch. It is not your device serial number, advertising identifier, or any other hardware identifier, and it is not linked to you.

You can clear your found progress at any time from the Profile tab. If you export a progress backup, that text is produced on your device and goes wherever you choose to send it. The app does not upload it.

Location

The app asks for location access only when you tap the locate button on the map. It uses your position while the app is open, to show where you are standing and to pick the nearest park. Your coordinates are used on your phone and are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. The app does not track your location in the background. You can deny or revoke this permission in your device settings, and every other part of the app keeps working.

Photos and camera

The app asks for photo library or camera access only when you choose to attach a picture to a sighting you are suggesting. It does not browse, scan, or read your photo library at any other time.

When you suggest a sighting

If you submit a suggested find, the app sends the following to a private review queue so that a person can check it:

  • The park, land, and attraction you selected.
  • The title, the description of where to look, the difficulty, and the location type you entered.
  • The photo you attached, if you attached one.
  • A name for credit, only if you typed one and ticked the box asking to be credited.
  • The random identifier described above, the app version, and whether you are on iOS or Android. These group submissions from the same install and limit spam.

Your email address, phone number, contacts, and real identity are never collected. Nothing you submit appears in the app until it has been reviewed and published as content.

When you report whether a find is still there

Reporting that a find is still visible, or has gone, sends only the entry you are reporting on and your answer. It is tied to an anonymous session identifier issued by the server so that one install counts as one report. It carries no personal information.

Where that data is held

Submissions and reports are stored with Supabase, a hosted database and file storage provider, in a private queue that only the maintainer can read. They are used solely to review and improve the guide. They are never sold, rented, or shared for advertising, and they are not used to build a profile of you.

How long it is kept

A submission is kept while it is under review. If it is published, the field note that results from it becomes part of the content of the app. If it is rejected, it and any attached photo are deleted. Reports on whether finds are still there are kept in aggregate to judge how reliable an entry is.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because it requires no account and asks for no personal details, there is normally nothing to collect.

Your choices

  • Reset your found progress from the Profile tab.
  • Revoke location, photo, or camera access in your device settings at any time.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Email the address below to ask that a sighting you submitted be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will also be noted in the release notes of the app.

Contact
